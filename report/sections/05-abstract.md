# 5. Abstract

MemForge is a deterministic operating-system memory-management simulator implemented in C17 with a companion WebAssembly deployment. The platform evaluates contiguous dynamic-partition allocation (First-Fit, Best-Fit, Worst-Fit, immediate coalescing, sliding compaction) and virtual demand paging (single-level page tables, virtual address translation, FIFO, LRU, Optimal replacement). By decoupling state mutations from policy decisions through a pure event-driven kernel, MemForge guarantees byte-identical execution traces for identical inputs and seeds.

Empirical evaluations across 30 synthetic workloads demonstrate significant divergence among contiguous placement policies under high memory pressure. At 90% memory utilisation, Best-Fit achieves a 91.4% ± 0.4% allocation success rate (127.7 ± 7.0 fragmentation failures), outperforming First-Fit (90.0% ± 0.6%, 148.6 ± 8.6 failures) and Worst-Fit (87.2% ± 0.7%, 192.0 ± 11.1 failures). Conversely, First-Fit incurs lower search overhead, scanning 3.21 ± 0.09 holes per request compared to 5.24 ± 0.15 for Best-Fit and 5.19 ± 0.15 for Worst-Fit. Compaction successfully eliminates external fragmentation, consolidating isolated free blocks to satisfy previously failed requests.

In demand paging evaluations over 2,000 memory references, Optimal replacement establishes the empirical lower fault bound, reducing faults from 1070.8 ± 13.9 at 2 frames to 53.2 ± 3.2 at 16 frames. Least Recently Used replacement substantially outperforms First-In, First-Out (138.9 ± 10.1 versus 253.1 ± 16.5 faults at 8 frames). Automated frame sweeps reproduce Belady's anomaly under FIFO, where faults increase from 9 to 10 when allocating a fourth frame. The simulation engine satisfies all eight verification acceptance criteria, maintains internal consistency across 17 automated test suites, and executes leak-free under dynamic address sanitizers and Valgrind memory analyzers.

**Keywords:** Memory Management, Deterministic Simulation, Contiguous Allocation, Demand Paging, Page Replacement Algorithms.
